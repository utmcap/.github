**UTMCAP is a click tracker for affiliate marketers, media buyers and small agencies.** Every click on your link is answered at the edge, close to the visitor, and recorded with its source, cost and outcome. When a conversion comes back by postback, UTMCAP ties it to the click that caused it, so you see profit and CPA by campaign, zone, placement, device or any token your traffic source passes.

## How it works

```
 Ad click  ──▶  UTMCAP edge  ──▶  Landing page / offer
                    │
                    ├─ records the click: source, cost, zone, device, bot check
                    │
 Network   ──▶  Postback  ──▶  matched to that click  ──▶  profit, ROI, CPA
```

1. **Add a traffic source.** Its click id, zone and cost macros are filled in for you.
2. **Add an offer.** The postback URL for your affiliate network is ready to copy.
3. **Run traffic.** Each click is answered at the edge and recorded; each conversion lands on the click that earned it.
