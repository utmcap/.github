<h3 align="center">Know what every click cost you.</h3>

UTMCAP is a click tracker for affiliate marketers, media buyers and small agencies. Every click on your link is answered at the edge, close to the visitor, and recorded with its source, cost and outcome. When a conversion comes back by postback, UTMCAP ties it to the click that caused it.

- **Priced per click, not per event.** Free up to 100,000 clicks a month, then from $29 for 1M clicks.
- **Bot filtering on every plan**, with the reason kept on each filtered click.
- **Built-in templates** for push, pop, native, search and social traffic sources, and postback guides for 39 affiliate networks.
- **A public API** (Growth plan and up) with read-only or read-write keys and an OpenAPI file.
